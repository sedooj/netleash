# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks, spam, demands for support or features, and posting
other people's personal data are not acceptable.

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock or delete comments, issues and pull requests, and block users, at their sole
discretion and without notice. Decisions about what is merged are the maintainer's alone.

Report problems with a participant's behaviour using GitHub's *Report content* feature.
